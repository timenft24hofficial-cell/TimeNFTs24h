The Lore

A day only happens once.

Every TimeNFTs24h collection covers exactly one calendar day. Each of its 1,440 tokens is engraved with the minute it was claimed — a permanent, unrepeatable record of when you showed up.

ORIGIN

One day, sealed

Mint opens at 00:00 UTC and closes at 00:00 UTC the next day. Whatever minutes remain unclaimed at the close are burned, not carried over.

MECHANIC

The minute is the trait

You don't pick a minute — the dial assigns the next open one when you claim. Your token's core trait is simply: which minute got you.

RECORD

Nothing resets it

Tokens live on-chain with their original claim timestamp locked in the metadata. No new mint, migration, or "day two" can touch it.

Rarity

Rarity is just what time it was.

Six bands split the day. Rarity follows band length — the fewer hours a band covers, the fewer minutes exist inside it.

Roadmap

Four stages of the day.

The roadmap borrows the collection's own clock — each stage is named for a time of day, not a step number.

Dawn

The window opens

The 24-hour mint goes live. The dial starts empty and fills minute by minute as claims come in.

Noon

The day is sealed

At 00:00 UTC the window closes for good. Unclaimed minutes are burned; the day's 1,440 are final.

Dusk

Holder hours

Claimants unlock a private channel and first look at how their minute's band performed against the rest of the day.

Midnight

Next day, new dial

A future day's dial opens, decided by the current holders — same mechanic, a new 1,440 minutes.
